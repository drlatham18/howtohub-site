Privacy Policy

HowToHub's publishing tool ("YouTube How-To Factory") is an internal application used only by the channel owner to upload videos to the channel's own YouTube account and to read that account's own analytics.

  • It accesses only the channel owner's own YouTube account, with the owner's consent, via Google's YouTube Data and YouTube Analytics APIs.
  • It does not collect, store, or share any information about viewers or any third party.
  • Google OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer and are never transmitted anywhere except to Google's APIs.
  • No advertising, tracking, or analytics run on this website.
